## Why

`evaluation.make_read_canaries` now takes a required keyword `pools`: the manifest's `data.pools` pins
({bench: {"path", "sha256"}}, naming at least the pilot's `protocol.ORDER`). When the record is made it reads the
pinned files (each must still hash to its pin) and never chooses a test text that occurs verbatim in the same public
field of a training-side pool item (registrar decision PR-C31-3 (1a)); the record gains `skipped` {bench: count} and
`training_pools` {bench: pin sha256 or None}, and `load_canaries` refuses a read record without them.
`run_unit_spec` passes `manifest["data"]["pools"]`. PR-C31-2's fixture line
`evaluation.make_read_canaries(setting_.root, None, run=..., evidence=FIXTURE, panel=canary_panel)` therefore needs
`pools=`. The e2e's Setting holds loaded items (`pools={SB: sb_items, OQ: OQ_ITEMS}`), so the fixture writes the two
pools as files outside the run root and pins them, as `export-pools` / `make-manifest` do on the real path.

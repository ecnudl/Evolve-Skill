# Ticket C2-3 → registrar: readings of registration §5 in `step.py` to confirm, and what it does not wire

From C2, 2026-10-22. Registration §5.4-§5.7 and §6.4 J3; Codex F22; tickets A9-3, C1-2 and C1-3.

1. **§5.5 (a), "S' 的故障率 > 2%".** C2 reads this as the share of S' self-test cells that have either a fault of a
   procedure the step adds or revises, or a re-execution `unknown_fault`. This is J3's rule (`acceptance.py:356-365`,
   Codex F22). Faults of inherited procedures happen in S as well. They are recorded as `inherited_fault_cells` and
   never block. In T-STEP-1, the parent's year-column procedure faulted on every generated table in both arms. Under
   the literal reading, every candidate would have been blocked there, whatever its change.
2. **§5.5, the second proposal.** After a block, the engineer's second call (unit `engineer-2`) gets the self-test as
   `previous` counts:
   {decision: not_accepted, faults: {selftest_fault_cells}, format: {bench: {worse_items}}}.
3. **§5.7.** At freeze, the describer writes one text per procedure that the step adds or revises. Earlier procedures
   keep their frozen text. A revision has a new id, and `package.with_` drops the old id's describer entry.
4. **§5.4 / §5.5.** If a step ends without a surviving change, or after a second block, its acceptance slice is never
   registered. Nothing is consumed, and the next step on that bench cuts those items again.
5. **§5.6.**
   - `mde_registered` is None. Ticket A9-3 asks A1 to register a per-bench MDE.
   - `freshness` = `route_b_fresh_method_side_reused` (plan §2.6).
   - S_dup runs when this step's generate unit admitted the bench's generator and k > 1.
   - G_acc is built from the frozen specs on the g_acc research half (C1-3).
   - The decision is written once in the accept unit's attempt directory, so a redo never collides. `sealed.json` is
     reused after `donors.verify`.
6. **Not wired in `step.py`.** These belong to the formal chain or the recommended pilot:
   - SearchQA's acceptance slice from the tail supply: `_slices` refuses a bench without an acceptance slice in its
     pool.
   - ALFWorld's selection path: the generate unit records mode "selection" and no generator.
   - SpreadsheetBench's shortcut probe (C1-2): until it exists, a SpreadsheetBench generated set is never admitted.
   - LiveMath: needs `Services.checker`.

# Ticket C5-4 → A6 (`solver.py`), A8 (`cells.py`): carry the sandbox's restarts and removal latencies into the records

From C5, 2026-10-22. Safety review finding 5 ("Report P1/P2/P3 by kind and arm in S2") and finding 3 ("record the
latency").

`sandbox.client.Worker` now records:
- `restarts`: one `{"cause": "oom" | "gone" | "start_failed", "container", "procedure"}` per container it replaced
  without dropping a procedure. After an OOM kill or a vanished container, the call was retried once in a fresh
  container. A failed start was retried once.
- `cleanups`: one `{"container", "seconds"}` per confirmed removal.

These events are neither procedure faults nor P4, so today they leave no trace in a cell record. Request: copy both
lists, or their counts, into the shell/cell record when a worker closes (`solver.solve` and `cells.run_cell`), so that
S2 can report them by kind and arm. The lists hold no model text: container names, procedure ids and numbers only.

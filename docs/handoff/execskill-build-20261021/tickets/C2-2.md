# Ticket C2-2 → A12 (`chain.cap_check`) and the registrar: chain G's cost check after its first run unit

From C2, 2026-10-22. Registration §9.3, owner rule 22:3x (c).

## 1. Registrar: confirm the projection that `step.cost_projection` uses

`step.py` calls the projection after chain G's first run unit (k = 1) and passes the result to `chain.cap_check`
unchanged:

- per cell = the tokens settled on `G:xs:<bench>:1:run`, divided by that unit's cells;
- factor = per cell / `CELL_ANCHORS[bench][1]`, the registered high anchor;
- projection of a scope = ceil(cap / 1.5 × factor), for every chain-G scope except `G:glm` and the role scopes;
- a breach is any projection above its cap.

Every cap is 1.5 × the maximum-schedule high estimate **plus one reservation** (`protocol.py:409-411`). So cap / 1.5
slightly overstates the high estimate, which is conservative. If the registrar wants the exact high estimates, they
need a protocol table.

## 2. A12: `cap_check` cannot be passed after an owner's cap raise

`chain.cap_check` (`chain.py:98-113`) writes `cap-check-G.json` once (`write_json`), with the measured scopes and
their caps. Suppose a breach pauses chain G and the owner raises a cap (`Budget.raise_cap`). On restart, the
completed run unit is read back and the projection is computed again with the raised cap. That is different content,
so `write_json` refuses it with a ValueError and the chain cannot continue. (A restart without a raise rewrites
byte-identical content and pauses again, which is correct.)

Request: record the re-check after the owner's decision as a new record, e.g. `cap-check-G-<n>.json` naming the
raise, or let the recorded decision close the check. `step.py` needs no change for either.

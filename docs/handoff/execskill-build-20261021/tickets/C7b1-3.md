## What happens

qualification before every SpreadsheetBench score. That qualification lives in `runtime.recalculation` of
`/data/coevo5-20261019-v3-run-manifest.json`, at `/data/coevo5-20261019-v3-sheet-q23-f/qualification.json`. Its
`protocol.json` records the engine identity.

- the path is `str(Path(__file__))` in `sheet_recalc_v7.Recalculator.identity`;
- the paths recorded are `/data/coevo5-20261019-v3-source-f/skillopt/continual_eval/sheet_recalc{,_v6,_v7,_worker}.py`, `spreadsheet_compat.py`, `envs/spreadsheetbench/evaluator.py` and `skill_validation/sandbox.py`.

From any other checkout, `scripts/replay_sheet_v6.py:133` raises `ValueError("Engine/source/image changed")`. That
includes `/data/execskill-dev` and the planned snapshot `/data/execskill-20261021-source`, even with byte-identical
files. `backends.score` then returns `unknown: native_scoring_input_error:ValueError`.

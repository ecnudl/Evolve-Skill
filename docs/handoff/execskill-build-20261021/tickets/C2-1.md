## 1. Not delivered by C2: the manifest adapters of A13-1 (items 1-3)

C2 ran out of time for these adapters. `step.py` is complete and tested. What remains:

- **`chain.run(manifest, chain=C)`.** Today the signature is `chain.run(setting, chain, *, learn)`, and it takes a
  `units.Setting`. The missing pieces are two builders, `setting_of(manifest, chain)` and
  `services_of(manifest, chain)`. With them the call becomes
  `chain.run(setting, chain, learn=functools.partial(step.learn, services=services))`.
- **The `Setting` built from the manifest:**
  - `run`, `root`, `shared`, `evidence`;
  - `pools`: each bench's supply in its fixed order;
  - `cards` and `runtime`;
  - `clients(model, bench)`: `client.solver_client(model, config=manifest.solver_config(m, chain, bench))`. With
    `bench` None it returns the GLM-5.3 role client;
  - `budget = Budget(budgets.ledger_root(root, chain))`, the chain's OWN ledger. `chain.run` refuses any other ledger
    (`budgets.own_ledger`);
  - `slots = slots.pools(SLOT_ROOT)`;
  - `sandbox`: the `Worker` keywords (`manifest.limits_of(m)`, docker, scratch, image);
  - `branch` and `longest` for chain D;
  - `extra = {"verify": lambda: manifest.verify_unit(m)}`. `step.py` calls `chain.check_pins` before every unit.
- **The `step.Services`:**
  - `registry = Registry(HISTORY_ROOT, evidence=...)`;
  - `sources`: `research.Sources` over the frozen archive (§7.2);
  - `workers`: `sandbox.client.with_worker` bound to the Setting's sandbox options;
  - `checker` (LiveMath) and `engine` (SpreadsheetBench), when those steps exist;
  - `item_workers = None`, so the step derives it from `workers` and mounts a SpreadsheetBench item's public workbook;
  - `clock` and `sleep` keep their defaults.
- **`step.run_unit_spec` and `evaluation.run_unit_spec`.** The current `chain.run` runs every step unit in its own
  process (`step.learn`). The only child it starts is the test read (`chain.evaluate` → `evaluate-unit`), so this
  design needs no `step-unit` child. If the owner wants one (A13-1 item 5), it takes {chain, k, bench} and calls
  `step.run_step`. That function reads every completed unit back and runs the next one.
- **`readout.report(manifest, out=)`.** C2 did not touch it.

  ledger, inside `chain.run`.

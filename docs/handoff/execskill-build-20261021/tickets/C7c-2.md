# Ticket C7c-2 → integrator (acceptance.py owner), owner decision #15: a paused acceptance redoes the WHOLE round

From C7c, 2026-10-22. Found while building S4 and S-E6. No code changed.

## What the code does

`step._accept` (step.py, the `work(attempt)` of the accept unit) calls `acceptance.dispatch(...)` on every attempt.
`acceptance.dispatch` (acceptance.py:225-250) runs `run(block, jobs)` for EVERY block of `schedule(sealed)`, in
order, each time. A redo (attempt-2 after the owner's `recovery.json`) therefore re-runs all blocks in its new
attempt directory: it re-runs the blocks that completed in attempt-1 too, and re-waits OfficeQA's 2-hour block gap.
The S4 rehearsal shows this: the drill paused attempt-1 inside block 1, and attempt-2 re-ran the whole 40-item round.

## What the registration says

- §9.3: "每次重做用自己的子作用域（`…:accept:attempt-N`），重做预留单列；SearchQA、OfficeQA、ALFWorld 的验收分块，
  暂停只重做一块" (each redo has its own sub-scope; a pause redoes ONE block).
- Plan §5 recovery: "a redo re-dispatches the whole block … no splicing across attempts (owner decision #15 may
  change this)".
- The caps follow the same rule: `…:accept:attempt-N` = min(kind cap, redo reserve), and the redo reserve is
  "the largest single acceptance block cap" (calc T6; registration line 1569).

## Why it matters before the pilot

At the maximum schedule, a whole-round redo of a multi-block acceptance does not fit its attempt sub-scope.
Example: OfficeQA accept cap 29.5M = 1.5 x ~19.5M (max-schedule high) + R. The attempt-2 cap is 14.8M (the redo
reserve), so a whole-round redo pauses on `…:accept:attempt-2`. A one-block redo fits.
S-E6 (`smoke.se6_projection`) prices `…:accept:attempt-N` as ONE block (cells / `SLICES[bench]["blocks"]`), as
registered. So S-E6 passing does NOT cover the current whole-round behaviour.

## Choices (owner / integrator)

1. Implement the registered one-block redo. `dispatch` resumes at the paused block, and the decision reads the
   completed blocks' cell records from the earlier attempt. This is block-level reuse across attempts, so it needs
   owner decision #15 (splicing).
2. Keep the whole-round redo. Re-register the attempt-N caps and the redo reserve at whole-round size in v1.1 before
   the pilot, and change the S-E6 pricing to match (`smoke._scope_cells`, C7c's code).

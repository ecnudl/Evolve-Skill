# Ticket C7b1-1 → C7b-2 (smoke.py) and C6 (manifest.py): S0-derived limits versus the smoke specification's start limits

From C7b-1 on 2026-10-22. It concerns registrar decision A5-1, registration §10 S0, and ticket C6-3.

## The conflict

Three pieces of code disagree when S0 derives limits above the start values:

- `smoke.sandbox_selftest(out, *, item_files, ...)` records the limits it measured under. These are the start values `sandbox.LIMITS`.
- `smoke.make` copies those limits into the specification's `sandbox.limits`.
- `manifest._smoke_pools` requires the manifest's limits to equal the specification's limits.

A5-1 derives the registered limits from the S0 measurements:

- RLIMIT_AS is max(2048 MiB, 2 × the largest worker VSZ, rounded up to 256 MiB).
- The frame cap is max(1 MiB, 2 × the largest public view, rounded up to 1 MiB).

Suppose a measurement pushes either value above its floor: a worker VSZ over 1 GiB, or a public view over 512 KiB. Then
the derived limits differ from the start values, and the smoke manifest cannot be made. For that reason, `s0.collect`
fails its measurements row whenever the derived limits differ from the self-test limits. It then writes no pins, so the
operator is stopped at S0 and does not reach `make-manifest`.

## Evidence that this is latent today

In the throwaway real run of 2026-10-22, both values stayed at the floors. The worker VSZ was 174,140 KiB. The largest
training public view was 30,116 B (OfficeQA); the workbook p99, 599,328 B, does not enter the frame cap.

## What is needed

Either of these resolves it:

- `sandbox_selftest` takes `limits=` and is re-run under the S0-derived limits before `smoke-make`.
- The registrar rules that the specification's limits are the S0-derived limits, so `smoke.make` takes them from `<root>/s0/s0-limits.json`.

Until then, the S0 measurements row enforces equality.

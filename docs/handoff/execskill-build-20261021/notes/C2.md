## Public API

`skillopt/execskill/step.py`:

- `Services(registry, sources, workers, item_workers=None, checker=None, engine=None, clock=time.time,
  sleep=time.sleep)`: what a step uses besides `units.Setting`.
  - `workers(package, *, kind, hashseed=0, inputs_dir=None)` is the engineer's, the detectors' and the replays'
    worker factory.
  - `item_workers(package, item)` is the router's and the donors' factory. When it is None, it is derived from
    `workers` and mounts a SpreadsheetBench public workbook for acceptance items and run-slice donors.
- `StepRecord(package, record)`.
- `run_step(setting, chain, bench, k, parent, *, services, chain_root=None) -> StepRecord`.
  - Raises `cells.Pause` or `chain.Terminated`.
  - A finished step (`step.json`) is read back without a model call.
- `learn(setting, chain_root, chain, k, bench, parent, *, services) -> (package, step record)`: the form `chain.run`
  calls, e.g. `functools.partial(learn, services=...)`.
- `cost_projection(setting, chain, bench, k, cells) -> {"ok", "per_cell", "anchor_high", "scopes": {scope: {"cap",
  "projected"}}}`: §9.3 for chain G after its first run unit, passed to `chain.cap_check`. Ticket C2-2.
- `selftest_verdict(cells, sets, changed) -> {"fault_cells", "inherited_fault_cells", "candidate_cells",
  "fault_share", "fault_blocks", "sets": {bench: {"items", "format_gap", "worse", "better", "p_sign", "blocks",
  "accuracy"}}, "blocked"}`: §5.5, computed from the cell records alone.

`skillopt/execskill/budgets.py`:
- `ledger_root(root, chain) -> Path` (`budgets.py:58`): `<root>/shared/ledger-G`, `<root>/shared/ledger-D`, and the
  smoke's `<root>/shared/ledger`.
- `own_ledger(setting, chain) -> Budget` (`:66`): ConfigurationError unless `setting.budget.root` is the chain's own

### What one step does, and what it writes

Each sub-step is a copied unit: `units.run_unit` at `<chain root>/s<k>-<bench>/<name>`, under `chain.heavy`, with
identity {unit, chain, k, bench, parent}.

- **Before the first unit:** the slices are sealed once in `slices.json` (record `step-slices`). Items another unit of
  this chain holds, and the smoke's items, are excluded.
- **`run`:**
  - the solver endpoint is built first, so a refusal comes before anything is registered;
  - then register, dispatch, `rounds.run_round` (S, plus C when firing is ≥ `ENTRY_READING_SHARE`), `rounds.guard`
    and decide;
  - the descriptive S - C reading is recorded;
  - for chain G at k = 1, `cost_projection` is passed to `chain.cap_check`.
- **`diagnose`:**
  - `detectors.split` → `examiner.detector_payload` (write half) → `examiner.detector_write`;
  - `detectors.seal_candidates` writes `candidates.json` in the attempt;
  - then `detectors.validate` and `detectors.detector_records`;
  - the library holds the detectors accepted by this chain's earlier diagnose units on the same bench.
- **`generate`** (benches with families only; SearchQA and ALFWorld record their mode):
  - the policy, then research through `research.Researcher` (scope `examiner:research`);
  - `research.split` is sealed as `research-split.json` in the attempt;
  - `generator_write` → `build`;
  - up to three band probes, each adjustment being a new `generator_write`;
  - the shortcut probe (None on SpreadsheetBench, C1-2), `gates` and `generator_record`.
- **`engineer`:** `engineer.Context` gets:
  - this chain's run-unit trajectories;
  - references for the failures of the parent's cells only (never on ALFWorld);
  - detector counts of the parent's cells;
  - `previous` taken from the last step record.
  The candidate is saved with `package.save`.
- **`selftest`, `engineer-2`, `selftest-2`:** §5.5 with two passes.
- **`accept`:**
  - register A (and `accept_probe`);
  - `acceptance.prepare` with `dup` = generator admitted and k > 1, G_acc from the frozen specs on the g_acc half,
    `freshness` = FRESHNESS and `mde=None`. A redo reuses `sealed.json` after `donors.verify`;
  - then `round_packages`, `runner`, `dispatch` (with the services' clock and sleep), `decide`;
  - `acceptance.write` puts the decision in the attempt directory, then `settle` runs.
- **`freeze`:** for each procedure the step adds or revises, its note lengths are replayed on the run slice and
  `describer.describe` is called (engineer scope); then `with_describer` and `save`. When nothing was accepted, S'
  stays archived.
- **At the end:** `step.json` (record `step`) is written once. It carries the decision, its hash, the J3 and J4
  counts, and every unit's record hash.

## Deviations, with reasons

1. **§5.5 (a) fault share.** Only faults of the procedures the step adds or revises count, plus re-execution
   `unknown_fault` (J3's rule). Inherited faults are reported apart. In T-STEP-1, an inherited procedure faulted in
   both arms on every generated table; the literal reading would block any candidate. Ticket C2-3 item 1.
2. **A step that ends before the acceptance never registers its acceptance slice.** Nothing is consumed (§5.4). An
   earlier draft registered and released the slice, which recorded a routing that never happened.
3. **The describer at freeze covers only the step's new and revised procedures.** Earlier ones keep their frozen
   text. Ticket C2-3 item 3.
4. **The second proposal is a separate unit, `engineer-2`,** whose `previous` is the self-test's counts. Then comes
   `selftest-2`. Ticket C2-3 item 2.
5. **The acceptance decision is written in the accept unit's attempt directory**, not `<unit root>/decision/`, so a
   redo cannot collide with a write-once file. `sealed.json` stays at the unit root and is reused after
   `donors.verify`.
6. **`mde_registered` is None.** No per-bench MDE constant exists yet (ticket A9-3).
7. **`cost_projection` is an implementation-time reading of §9.3.** Ticket C2-2 item 1.
8. **The fixtures reuse B3's parent package and B4's YEARS change.** The test pool is OfficeQA pages 40-119, so no
   YEARS note contains a run-slice reference answer, as in B4.

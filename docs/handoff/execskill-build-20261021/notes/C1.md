**Detectors (§5.2, §7.1)**

- `detector_payload(halves, *, bench, library=(), workers=None) -> dict`. Shows the write half only, through
  `detectors.examiner_diagnosis`. Each trajectory carries:
  - the masked view (`trajectories.masked_view(..., purpose="examiner")`);
  - `passed`, the registered right/wrong bit of §4.3;
  - the mechanical categories (`detectors.mechanical_masked`);
  - `covered_by`: the accepted detectors that fire, run in the sandbox through `detectors.evaluate`;
  - `residual`.

  Residual cells come first, then seeded order. The payload also has the category counts and the library's ids and
  descriptions. It is fitted to `ROLE_INPUT_BYTES`.
- `parse_detectors(reply) -> [(description, source)]`. Needs exactly one ```json manifest
  `{"detectors": [{"description"}]}` (at most 3) and one ```python block per entry. Otherwise it raises
  `Unparseable`.
- `detector_write(roles, payload, *, k, label, evidence) -> (candidates, sealed examiner-detectors record)`.
  - At most `DETECTOR_CALLS` `roles.plain` calls; the second goes only after an unparseable reply and carries
    `"unparseable"`.
  - Candidates are `detectors.Detector` objects with registrar ids `d-<bench>-<k>-<n>` (`fx-` for fixtures), skipping
    library ids.
  - A source refused by the static policy, or one without `detect(view)`, is dropped with its reason.
  - The record carries `candidates_sha256` (`detectors.candidates_sha256`). Sealing before the hold half and
    validation stay in `detectors`.

**Generation policy and research (§5.3, §7.2, owner rule (b))**

- `catalogue(bench)` returns the registered families.
- `outcome_counts(gate_records, *, research=(), generator_records=()) -> dict` counts:
  - research requests by kind and status;
  - sets made and admitted;
  - failed gates, with `no_items` for the truth gate;
  - items made and kept;
  - drops by reason;
  - band outcomes;
  - spec statuses per family;
  - generator statuses.

  It never includes an item id or a verdict (isolation review 21).
- `policy_payload(bench, *, previous=None, outcomes=None)` refuses SearchQA, non-aggregated outcomes (a key with
  "/" or "gen:") and any `FORBIDDEN_KEYS` key.
- `policy_revise(roles, payload, *, label, evidence) -> (policy, sealed examiner-policy record)`.
  - One `roles.ask` call. The policy is `{"bench", "families", "requests", "rationale"}`.
  - Requests are `family_material`, `parameter_choice` or (ALFWorld) `selection_hint`, at most 24.
  - A SpreadsheetBench concept must be a function name (`research.FUNCTION`).
  - An invalid reply falls back to the previous policy (`fallback: "previous"`), else to an empty one (`"empty"`).
- `research_requests(policy, researcher) -> [(request, research record)]` goes through `research.Researcher`. Its
  pre-dispatch leakage screen and ceilings apply.

**Specs and build**

- `usable(bench, materials)`, `researched_values(bench, materials)`.
- `generator_payload(bench, materials, *, sources=None, band=None)` shows the catalogue, the qualified materials of
  ONE research half, the researched in-range parameter values and the SpreadsheetBench source public views. `band`
  is the previous probe's band value; the call refuses an in-band probe or a third adjustment.
- `generator_write(roles, payload, *, label, evidence) -> (specs, sealed examiner-generators record)`.
  - At most `GENERATOR_CALLS` `roles.ask` calls.
  - Specs look like `{n, family, material, params[, source]}`, or `{n, selection}` on ALFWorld.
  - Dropped with a reason: `answer_field`, `keys`, `unknown_family`, `material_not_shown`, `source_not_shown`,
    `params`, `format`, and ALFWorld `Drop` reasons.
- `Generated(generator, bench, results)` with `.items`.
- `build(specs, materials, *, bench, generator, set_name, seed, evidence, sources=None, checker=None, engine=None,
  out_dir=None) -> Generated`. It uses `generators.generate` and enforces a cap of 10 items per finding across specs
  (`finding_exhausted`).

**Gates (§7.2) and records**

- `probe_items(generated, *, generator, purpose)` returns at most 30 items in seeded order.
- `shortcut_variant(item)`: OfficeQA keeps the question only, LiveMath the options only. SpreadsheetBench raises
  `ConfigurationError` (ticket C1-2).
- `truth(results)`.
- `band(rounds, item_ids)`: one to three probes. The last probe decides; `direction` is too_easy or too_hard;
  unknown counts as not passed.
- `shortcut(cells, item_ids, *, bench)`: two runs per item. Items right twice are dropped; a dropped share above
  1/4 rejects the set. None means `not_run`.
- `labels(generated, *, bench)`: truth must come from registrar fixed code (`checked_by` → kind), and on LiveMath
  each letter must be 20 % ± 5 %.
- `gates(bundle, probe_cells, *, evidence) -> sealed generator-gates record` with `failed`, `admitted`, `kept` and
  `kept_sha256`.
- `generator_record(gates_record, specs, *, rounds=(), dup=None, evidence) -> sealed generator record` (plan §2.5).
  Status is `not_admitted`, `admitted`, or `validity.status(...)` after rounds.
- `g_acc(items, astar, *, bench, generator) -> [{"id", "source_item"}]` is the `generate` value for
  `acceptance.prepare` (ticket A9-3). It pairs the kept G_acc items one to one with A* items in seeded order, at
  most `G_ACC[bench]` (ticket C1-3).
- `selection(spec, pool_views, materials, *, n, seed_tag, evidence) -> sealed selection-validity record`, for
  ALFWorld. It measures selection validity only and is never an examiner claim.

## Deviations from the plan (with reasons)

1. **Return shapes.** `detector_write`, `generator_write` and `policy_revise` return `(value, sealed record)`, not a
   bare list. The §7.2 report needs attempts, drop reasons, call counts and request hashes. This follows the
   `engineer.run` convention.
2. **A "generator" is not model code.**
   - The plan's single generator object (family + parameter ranges + research requests) is split:
     `policy_revise` carries the families and research requests, and `generator_write` carries the parameter specs
     written after research on one research half.
   - Specs are JSON over `generators.FAMILIES`. No model-written code runs for generation, and every truth comes
     from fixed registrar code (Codex F8).
   - `research_requests` therefore takes the policy, not a generator (research precedes the specs, owner rule (b)).
3. **The caller runs the probe cells.** `gates`, `band` and `shortcut` read sealed cell records.
   - A band adjustment is a new `generator_payload(band=...)` + `generator_write` + `build`, at most twice.
     `band()` refuses a fourth probe.
   - Charging the probe cells to `...:examiner` is step.py's job.
4. **Extra status.** The generator record has status `not_admitted` in addition to the plan's
   admitted / validated / refuted / not_evaluable.
5. **Helpers beyond the plan text.**
   - `outcome_counts`: the only allowed input of the policy's outcomes (isolation review 21).
   - `g_acc`: the `acceptance.prepare(generate=...)` contract (ticket C1-3 states the reading).
   - `selection`: ALFWorld's selection-validity record.
6. **Bench scope.** SearchQA is refused for policy and generation, since it does not generate (§7.2); detectors
   accept all five benches.
7. **SpreadsheetBench shortcut variant impossible.** The copied solver has no workbook-free view, so it raises
   `ConfigurationError` (ticket C1-2). As a result no SpreadsheetBench generated set can be admitted yet.

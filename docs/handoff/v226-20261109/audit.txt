Candidate containment
---------------------

main-v10 guards candidate engineering checks, replay/routing, train, confirmation,
split, self-test and acceptance preparation, including card phases. One unexpected
candidate exception records exactly kind/exception/where, without message or
item IDs, and rejects that candidate. A signature is exception@module:function.
All phases/resumes of that candidate share one stable key and count it once.
An append-only, hash-linked sealed journal makes the counters recoverable.
Two consecutive candidates with the same signature, or an incumbent/parent
signature, rethrow the original exception for the unchanged unit pause mapping.
Counterfactual reference packages containing new procedures remain candidate
work, while actual incumbent and inherited solo replays are protected parent work.

Pause/termination, declared configuration/policy/provenance/sealing/leakage/canary
contracts, budget, machine/network errors, pre-proposal prompt/data work and
paired paid dispatch remain hard. Normal sandbox procedure fault results are
still ordinary procedure faults, not reclassified host exceptions. The known
defects above have direct fixes and are not hidden by containment.

Completed step.json contains candidate_errors signature counts and safe
candidate_error_events; imported step 5 has explicit empty fields. Readouts
include summed signature counts and a total. Before a step completes, the sealed
journal and paused unit status carry its counts: step.json remains the existing
write-once completion marker and is not fabricated for an interrupted step.

Tests cover isolated, repeated, parent/system, sealed-journal corruption,
resume deduplication, no-proposal intervening rounds, safe writer feedback,
card continuation, train/confirm/accept/self-test continuation, and readout sums
in test_execskill_v226_candidates.py, _procedure_errors.py and _card_errors.py.

# coevo5 正式登记的 Codex 设计审查第 1 轮（2026-10-16 19:32，结论 NOT ACCEPTABLE）

> 审查对象：[正式登记](coevo5-registration-20261016.md)。原样保存 Codex 的结论，供下一轮修订逐条处理。
> 命令：`codex exec -m gpt-6-astra -c model_reasoning_effort='"ultra"' --sandbox read-only`。

1. **MUST-FIX — The claimed 3–4% false-acceptance rate is unsupported (§5).** In the [registration](../../docs/handoff/coevo5-registration-20261016.md?plain=1#L72), failure-targeted, adaptively proposed rules are compared with fixed irrelevant instructions, including controls inserted in different locations. Equal effectiveness does not imply exchangeability. Even under exchangeability, strict ties make the probability of winning **at most** 1/5; multiplying two round probabilities additionally requires independence that fresh seeds do not establish. Persistent rule/item effects survive reruns. **Change:** define the null hypothesis, match control placement and construction, and calibrate the complete selection procedure. Otherwise call this a heuristic gate and remove the numerical error guarantee. Existing probe seeds shuffle execution order; they do not seed model inference.

2. **MUST-FIX — The “no harm” conditions explicitly allow harm (§5, conditions 2–4).** A rule can lose several items against the parent yet pass because one placebo loses more. Likewise, matching the worst placebo’s format-violation count does not establish safety. Summed regression performance permits improvement on one bench to conceal damage on another. **Change:** anchor harm limits to the parent, specify tolerable margins and uncertainty criteria for each bench, and retain placebos as diagnostics. Small panels—four ALFWorld episodes or six SpreadsheetBench tasks—must yield “undetermined” when they cannot support the registered safety claim.

3. **MUST-FIX — Gate multiplicity is unaddressed (§5).** There are up to 25 proposed rules per chain and 100 across four chains, plus scope changes and composition decisions. Even a genuine independent 4% per-rule error rate would imply approximately 64% probability of at least one false acceptance per chain; this is an illustration, not an estimate for the dependent procedure. **Change:** define the error family and register a justified sequential allocation or full-pipeline calibration. Distinguish per-rule error from chain-level error and from subsequent test-set evidence.

4. **MUST-FIX — Failure-defined triggers need an explicit fresh-parent comparison and exact panel construction (§5).** Regression to the mean would inflate improvement if the original failed executions supplied the parent scores. Fresh contemporaneous parent executions on the same frozen triggers can address that particular problem; repeated executions still cannot demonstrate generalization beyond examples used to propose the rule. “Remaining items” also conflicts with “all 74” OfficeQA items and “all 57” ALFWorld episodes. **Change:** prohibit reuse of diagnostic executions as gate comparators; freeze trigger IDs before gate results; specify trigger/transfer repeat counts, complement construction, overlaps, deduplication and small-panel behavior.

5. **MUST-FIX — Pairwise exclusion and 90% coverage do not protect against informative missingness (§§5, 8).** Different arm-specific exclusions make ranked sums refer to different items. A one-item observed gain can be overturned by the permitted missing outcomes. This is concrete: the [10/16 ledger](../../docs/results-and-lessons.md?plain=1#L140) records arm-dependent `finish_length` unknowns. **Change:** distinguish solver noncompletion from infrastructure failure, freeze comparable item/run support, report differential missingness and require conservative missing-outcome bounds for acceptance. Specify completion-aware outcomes or sensitivity analyses without silently changing official scoring. Apply an explicit missingness policy to final inference too. Freeze SpreadsheetBench eligibility independently of candidate outcomes; 90% coverage on a six-item panel requires all six.

6. **MUST-FIX — Composition and scope changes bypass parts of the advertised gate (§5).** The composite only passes conditions 2–4, so individually beneficial rules can cancel each other’s trigger or transfer benefits and still be accepted together. A shared rule demoted to local placement has not passed both rounds in its final placement. Choosing the largest confirmation gain after composition failure adds another selection step. **Change:** define composite trigger/transfer panels and test the complete applicable benefit-and-harm criteria on the final rendered Skill. Register how placement changes are rechecked and use a predetermined eligible-rule fallback or explicitly account for the additional selection.

7. **SHOULD-FIX — Establish power before interpreting rejection (§5).** The ledger reports effects around +1–3 items amid substantial execution and proposal noise. Requiring two strict wins over four controls may again detect large instruction effects while missing realistic incremental effects. Discreteness matters: +1 on four triggers is 25 percentage points; +1 on six transfer probes is 16.7 points, with substantial ceiling and tie effects. **Change:** provide training-side simulations or historical calibration incorporating actual panel sizes, ties, dependence and missingness. Register minimum detectable effects and explicitly distinguish “no rule accepted” from “no useful effect.”

8. **MUST-FIX — The launch schedule contradicts both dependencies and test isolation (§12).** Stage 1 already requires SpreadsheetBench/ALFWorld regression executions; stage 0 evaluates all five benches; I-chain initialization requires all five instructions. These components therefore cannot be finished during the first three main stages. Current code provides components, not this complete experiment: [the probe](../../scripts/weak_solver_probe.py#L76) supports only SearchQA/OfficeQA and rejects test data, while [the stage runner](../../scripts/run_fivebench_g_stage.py#L57) excludes SpreadsheetBench/ALFWorld learning. **Change:** complete, review and freeze all adapters, scorers, instructions, baselines and analysis before main execution/test access. Keep the smoke training-only and outside confirmatory histories. Seal evaluation outputs against human as well as automated selection.

9. **MUST-FIX — Future-domain label access and “equal labeled budget” need a consistent contract (§§2, 5, 9).** Every stage consults labeled regression examples from future benches. Thus “not yet learned” means “not yet the focal training stage,” not unseen-domain transfer. Equal pool sizes also do not equalize actual label access, repeated scoring or computation. Merging old validation data into analyst inputs conflicts with the current project-status exclusion rule. **Change:** explicitly register the new split/access contract while preserving historical identities; either equalize future-domain access across baselines or narrow the comparison accordingly. Report available labels, unique labels consulted, generated labels, calls and tokens separately. Describe the method as using no **separate held-out validation partition**, not no labeled selection data.

10. **MUST-FIX — The answer-leakage check is insufficient and ambiguous (§4).** Banning reference-answer strings misses paraphrased facts, encoded mappings and source-specific shortcuts. Literal matching against common numbers or LiveMath’s A/B/C/D answers can also reject ordinary procedural language. **Change:** specify normalized, typed leakage checks with explicit generic-token handling; prohibit question-specific facts and answer mappings; keep provenance outside rendered instructions; and define a reproducible adjudication procedure. The checker must not consume test answers.

11. **MUST-FIX — Research sources and transfer-probe validation remain underspecified (§§4, 7).** Benchmark papers or documentation may contain benchmark examples despite the dataset-site blacklist. Exact quotation verifies textual provenance, not whether evidence supports an answer. Likewise, a correct expression does not prove that it matches the question or that a LiveMath option label is correct. Six rule-conditioned generated probes are selection material, not independent transfer evidence—a particularly important distinction given the recorded generated-versus-real discrepancy. **Change:** freeze permitted URLs/sections, document hashes, Wikipedia revisions, redirect filtering and search archives; require question–evidence–answer consistency; specify generation attempts, minimum surviving probes and shortfall handling. Freeze the search backend for this protocol.

12. **MUST-FIX — Claim 1’s noninferiority rule contradicts the blanket interval rule (§10).** A confidence interval of [−1, +1] percentage points establishes noninferiority against a −2-point margin while containing zero. Moreover, macro-average noninferiority does not establish that each bench is not worse, and the two-point margin lacks justification. **Change:** name and justify the macro-average estimand and margin; separate superiority, noninferiority and inconclusive outcomes; state whether both baseline comparisons must pass. Give the E-chain-versus-instruction “reaches its level” question its own noninferiority/equivalence rule—failure to detect a difference is insufficient.

13. **MUST-FIX — The proposed intervals omit learning-history uncertainty and the final claim family (§10).** One chain per model/start plus item bootstrap supports conditional statements about those realized Skills, not reproducible method performance. E/I chains and different solvers are not interchangeable replications. The [10/13 result](../../docs/results-and-lessons.md?plain=1#L184) already demonstrates why item-level and history-level uncertainty must be distinguished. **Change:** preregister independent complete-chain replications for methods and baselines, or narrow inference explicitly to these histories. Specify bootstrap units, source clustering where applicable, pairing across stages and shared references. Define a primary comparison hierarchy and multiplicity treatment for separately asserted claims across five benches and comparators. One run per item is not intrinsically invalid, but cannot separately establish solver repeatability.

14. **MUST-FIX — Forgetting, negative transfer and overall method failure are not operationally defined (§10).** The registration omits whether averages use final-stage or all-stage cells, signed declines or clipped losses, and equal bench or equal cell weighting. Gains can cancel damage under one interpretation but not another. A method that never updates obtains zero forgetting automatically. **Change:** write exact formulas and eligible indices, preserve paired trajectories in uncertainty calculations, and report acquisition/final accuracy alongside harm. Define the outcomes that leave each claim unsupported and what constitutes overall method success; gate acceptance, noninferiority to weak baselines and zero updates must not automatically establish useful evolution.

15. **MUST-FIX — Deepseek cannot receive “the same readings” without matched baselines (§§1, 9, 10).** SkillOpt/GEPA run only on GLM, so their results cannot establish deepseek noninferiority or comparative forgetting while holding the solver fixed. Keeping every method role on GLM also makes this a solver-change study with a common GLM designer. **Change:** either add matched deepseek baselines or restrict deepseek analyses to its own trajectories and instruction/No-Skill comparisons. Label the result cross-solver robustness, with the fixed method-role model clearly stated.

16. **SHOULD-FIX — The instruction comparison measures a five-instruction bundle (§§1, 3, 10).** I-chain begins with instructions for every domain, including future domains. Its final-minus-initial contrast is a valid incremental-learning comparison for that bundle, but does not establish superiority over each bench’s standalone instruction; bundle interference could weaken the reference. **Change:** freeze the complete initial text and limit claims to that comparator. Add standalone instruction measurements if claiming improvement over the strongest relevant instruction baseline. The evidence-selected fixed bench order should likewise limit conclusions to that order.

17. **MUST-FIX — The examiner panel is impossible for ALFWorld, and its downstream role is undefined (§6).** Splitting 57 one-run episodes by item leaves at most 29 B responses, not the required 40. Twenty errors may also be unavailable near ceiling. Separately, a bench-specific rubric revised after its sole learning stage cannot improve that stage’s earlier diagnoses. Adaptive format checks could alter what later gates call a violation. **Change:** register feasible sample sizes, class-shortfall behavior and the balanced-accuracy estimand; specify which revisions affect which later diagnoses. Freeze gate scoring/violation semantics separately from adaptive diagnostic rubrics, or precisely constrain their evolution.

18. **SHOULD-FIX — Examiner improvement is measured on its selection panel (§§6, 10).** B selects revisions and then supplies the reported improvement; accepted revisions therefore improve there by construction. Filtering generated probes similarly raises compliance with the filter without independently establishing quality. **Change:** report all attempted revisions and rejection outcomes, label B results as selection measurements, and use an independent audit if claiming improved examiner accuracy. Attribution of Skill gains to examiner evolution requires an appropriate ablation; otherwise keep that attribution descriptive.

19. **SHOULD-FIX — The proposed “format/content decomposition” is not a valid decomposition (§5).** Finding a reference answer anywhere can reward answer lists or contradictory answers; finding the correct option letter anywhere in LiveMath is especially permissive. Removing units or percent signs can erase substantive meaning. **Change:** call these lenient-match diagnostics, define unambiguous extraction and numerical normalization, and audit their failure modes. Report lenient-minus-official as a gap, without equating it with a causal formatting contribution. Keep the gate on frozen official scoring as proposed.

20. **SHOULD-FIX — Historical No-Skill reuse needs stronger controls (§9).** Matching nominal solver configuration and item IDs does not eliminate endpoint drift, runtime changes or time effects. Aggregate historical scores cannot support paired analysis. These previously inspected test sets also are not newly untouched confirmation sets. **Change:** prefer contemporaneous No-Skill measurements; otherwise freeze the reuse decision and exact per-item record manifest before new outcomes, verify rendered prompts/scorers/runtime identities, and label historical controls and prior test exposure. References must be shared only within the matching solver/configuration.

21. **MUST-FIX — Several promised frozen artifacts are absent (§§1–7).** The registration supplies neither the five fixed applicability sentences nor the eight fixed placebo texts. It also leaves unresolved which two QA formats SpreadsheetBench/ALFWorld use, how the 30-probe regression cap works, composition tie-breaking, no-accepted-rule behavior and combined-text overflow. **Change:** attach a hashed prelaunch manifest covering these choices, all prompts, initial instructions/rubrics, solver settings, sampling rules, cache identities and retry policies. Specify defaults before outcomes can influence their implementation.

22. **MUST-FIX — The budget and stop rule are not executable (§11).** Only chain totals are provided, yet stopping depends on exceeding a per-stage estimate. Costs depend on both gate rounds, parent/control reuse, composition, generated probes, failed requests and GLM method-role calls within deepseek chains. ALFWorld resends the growing Skill at each action, potentially up to 50 actions per episode in the [existing backend](../../skillopt/continual_eval/backends.py#L699). **Change:** supply per-stage call/token/currency estimates and hard limits, including upper scenarios and missing usage. Define exactly what stops and how incomplete chains enter reporting. The present totals are not sufficiently itemized to validate cost realism.

VERDICT: NOT ACCEPTABLE

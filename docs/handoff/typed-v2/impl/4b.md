## Choices

1. **Row keys.** The rows have the ten keys of DESIGN 3.2 / 4.1, plus typed-v1's `entry`, `source`, `kind` and
   `repairs`. That is 14 keys.
   - spec/engineer.md 3 says the typed-v1 keys stay. DESIGN lists the new shape but does not say they go, as it does
     say for the pair fields of `noise.json`.
   - The extra keys keep evidence that would otherwise exist only in the calls file: the text of a dropped or
     not-needed proposal and the reader's repair rows.
2. **Status `no_reply`, a seventh status.** A proposal call that delivered no reply gives one row (`n` None, `read`
   None, `format_retry` None, reason `no_reply`, as in typed-v1). The six statuses of the design cover only read
   records. `unreadable` would mix a missing reply into the reply-reading measure of D4.
3. **A whole-reply reading problem** gives one `unreadable` row:
   - `n` None and `read` None (there is no record);
   - `repairs` = the reader's reply-level rows;
   - reason `'unparseable: <code>: <sentence>'`.
4. **`not_needed` is not screened.** DESIGN 3.2 says "not screened, no call"; spec 1.3 item 11 says "screened once for
   the record". DESIGN wins. So `first_screen` is None and no id is taken.
5. **`first_screen`** is `{'passed', 'check', 'rules'}`.
   - For `policy`, `rules` holds the distinct rule ids, in the checker's order, of the violations
     `build_procedure` reports (its first five, the typed-v1 form).
   - For the other checks it is the check's rule name from `CHECK_WORDS`: `no_hook_function`, `structure` or
     `source`.
   - A passed first screen gives `{'passed': True, 'check': None, 'rules': []}`.
   - The full evidence of the failure (8 rows and `more`) is in `fix.screen_evidence`.
6. **The fix record has a fixed key set** (10 keys; see "Left for later steps"). Values are None or [] where nothing
   applies.
   - `request_sha256` is the call whose reply was read. That is the fix call's own retry when it was used; the
     original fix call is then `format_retry.first_request_sha256`. This matches `read_with_retry`, and the existing
     test assertion `lineage == fix.request_sha256` still holds.
   - An unreadable fix keeps `problem`, `read` and the reader's repair rows. Its `entry` and `source` are None.
7. **Fix request evidence** is computed in W from build_procedure's failure. `screen_evidence` re-runs the pure screen
   for `policy`. The `change` drops both `source` and its alias `code` (`replies.INLINE_KEYS`).
8. **`propose_programs` keeps its signature.** There is no `max_proposals` keyword: spec change 10 says the signature
   is unchanged, and the reader's limit `profiles.MAX_CHANGES` equals `MAX_PROGRAM_PROPOSALS` (4a pins it). A
   `require(len(records) <= MAX_PROGRAM_PROPOSALS)` guard keeps "at most 5 calls" true even if the two ever
   differ.
9. **The `verify()` memo is private** (`_prompt_verified`, `_PROMPT_CHECK`) and memoises only a success. No public
   name was added for it.
10. **Hooks-check details.**
    - The detail text is `defined: <defined hooks, comma separated>`, or `defined: none`.
    - After the policy screen passes, `require(bench in NOTE_HOOKS_BY_BENCH)` raises ValueError for an unregistered
      bench. Every caller passes one of the five.
11. **An explicit empty `changes` list** is a readable "no proposal" and gives no row. This also holds after a format
    retry; the retry is then visible only in the calls file.
12. **`kind_not_new` reason.** The row keeps typed-v1's `kind_not_new:<kind>`. The chain's decision reason
    `kind_not_new` is 4c's.
